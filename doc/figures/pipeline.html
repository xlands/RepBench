An open-source, reusable closed-loop pipeline for capability-representation data

Given any benchmark, the pipeline turns it into clean per-capability representation data via crawl → LLM audit → human review → probe test; gaps exposed by testing flow back to crawling, driving multiple iterations until convergence. The resulting data plugs into any representation / steering method.
Figure 1. The closed-loop data-synthesis pipeline. The orange/purple stages (LLM audit + human review) are the quality and cost core: 10 models vote on every text↔capability mapping via hidden-state agreement, and humans adjudicate suspected mismatches. The red dashed arc is the iteration loop — coverage gaps and bad clusters found by the probe test (stage 5) flow back to crawling, making the pipeline a repeatable engine. Once open-sourced, the community can feed in new benchmarks and obtain same-format clean data, which is method-agnostic: it drives DiffMean, PCA/LAT, CAA, linear probing, SAE, J-Lens, and ReFT-r1 alike.
