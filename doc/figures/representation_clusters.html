Reading. On raw per-text vectors every capability smears into one cloud — benchmark format and token statistics dominate, and the silhouette-vs-k curve climbs monotonically to the sweep ceiling (no interior optimum, no natural cluster count). After pooling each capability across its ≥2 benchmarks the nuisance variance cancels and a coarse discrete structure emerges: every model's silhouette now peaks at small k (4–15). We claim the emergence of coarse structure, not an exact cluster count; the discovered clusters do not reproduce the human family taxonomy (ARI ≈ 0.1), which is expected.
